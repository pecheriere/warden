warden-demo is a demo. It is not secure for real use.

Everyone on the machine counts as the owner, it serves plain HTTP on loopback,
and all its data is throwaway. Do not put real secrets near it, and do not use
it as a warden server.

What it shows: https://github.com/pecheriere/warden/blob/main/docs/demo.md
A real deployment: https://github.com/pecheriere/warden/blob/main/docs/deployment.md
